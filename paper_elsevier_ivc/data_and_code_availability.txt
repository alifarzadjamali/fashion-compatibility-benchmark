Data and code availability

Evaluation code, frozen configuration, protocol hashes, checkpoint revisions, and reproduction instructions are publicly available at https://github.com/alifarzadjamali/fashion-compatibility-benchmark.

Original Polyvore, IQON3000, and A100 images are not redistributed and must be obtained from their respective sources subject to their terms. Pretrained model weights are obtained from their original providers. Cached frozen embeddings, fitted binary artifacts, and raw per-example predictions are not included in the Git repository; reproduction requires running the documented extraction and evaluation pipeline.

Audit summaries, derived tables, figures, and statistical outputs are generated locally by the pipeline. Generated reports and manuscript files are excluded from the Git repository.
