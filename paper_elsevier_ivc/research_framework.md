# RepBench-Fashion research framework

Final framework size: **X = 4**.

Five earlier questions separated several analyses that are scientifically parts of the same inference. The revised framework keeps the prospective representation comparison distinct (RQ1), groups construct/model-boundary evidence together (RQ2), retains operational model selection as its own question (RQ3), and treats robustness as support for all conclusions while giving the validity audit an explicit question (RQ4). This produces exactly four gaps, objectives, questions, and contributions without causal language about scale, data, architecture, or training loss.

| Gap | Objective | Research question | Contribution | Main evidence | Discussion answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G1.** Contemporary released frozen representations have not been compared systematically for relational outfit compatibility under one downstream probe across leakage-audited corpora. | **O1.** Measure the shared-probe hierarchy and prospectively replicate it. | **RQ1.** Under an identical frozen-feature relational probe, how do seven checkpoints compare on item- and exact-image-disjoint Polyvore, and does the broad hierarchy replicate on IQON3000-Clean? | **C1.** A controlled seven-checkpoint benchmark that establishes a provenance-robust VLM-tier advantage and prospectively replicates it on IQON. | `main.tex`, Table 5 and Fig. 1; `reports/tables/final/main_polyvore_iqon_results.csv`; paired/group-aware statistics; provenance sensitivity. | The evaluated contrastive VLMs form the leading group; the broad ranking replicates and survives exclusion of Marqo and GR-Lite, with lower absolute IQON scores and a narrower retained-subset target. |
| **G2.** The construct and modelling boundaries among frozen transfer, task-specific contextual learning, fashion specialization, retrieval, and external human/expert judgments are insufficiently triangulated. | **O2.** Test those boundaries without treating heterogeneous published protocols as directly comparable. | **RQ2.** How far do the conclusions extend to A100, contextual learning, specialization contrasts, and retrieval-versus-compatibility ranking? | **C2.** Boundary triangulation using unchanged A100, an image-only OutfitTransformer adaptation, matched specialization contrasts, and exact-checkpoint LookBench overlap. | Figs. 2–3; A100 and baseline frozen tables; LookBench v3 Table/source; predefined contrasts. | A100 corroborates the family-level advantage; the contextual baseline is competitive but not dominant; specialization is recipe-dependent; retrieval ranks reverse and are not a safe compatibility-selection rule. |
| **G3.** Accuracy-only leaderboards omit calibration, efficiency, and provenance constraints. | **O3.** Compare operational selection criteria rather than declare one universal winner. | **RQ3.** Do discrimination, benchmark calibration, measured efficiency, and checkpoint provenance favour the same representation? | **C3.** Joint discrimination/protocol-calibration/compute/provenance reporting and conditional Pareto analysis. | Table 6; Figs. 4–5; calibration, efficiency, and provenance artifacts. | No. The axes prefer different checkpoints, and benchmark calibration cannot be transported to population prevalence. |
| **G4.** Compatibility conclusions may be fragile to leakage, false generated questions, IQON selection/dependence, construction seeds, feature width, learner choice, or label availability. | **O4.** Stress-test the conclusions with existing manifests, predictions, and frozen robustness outputs. | **RQ4.** Which conclusions survive the specified validity and robustness audits? | **C4.** Leakage-audited Polyvore and IQON protocols plus exact-collision, historical-bridge, retained-subset, dependence, seed, width, learner, compatibility-label, subgroup, failure, provenance, and traceability checks. | Figs. 6–8; Supplementary material; `artifacts/*audit*.json`; frozen robustness tables. | The broad hierarchy survives; the audits also expose bounded IQON selection/dependence, synthetic-negative, transformed-duplicate, and pretraining-contamination limitations. |

## Navigation invariant

The manuscript introduction defines G1–G4, O1–O4, and C1–C4. Section 3 states RQ1–RQ4 and contains the compact mapping table. Every Results subsection carries its RQ marker, and the Discussion contains exactly four explicit “Answer to RQ” subsections. The Conclusion reports the same four answers without adding a claim.
