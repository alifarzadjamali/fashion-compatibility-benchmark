# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone who
participates in this project, regardless of background, identity, experience, or personal
characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific in technical discussion.
- Assume good faith and make room for differing viewpoints.
- Discuss evidence and ideas rather than making personal remarks.
- Respect privacy, dataset access conditions, and intellectual-property boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, deliberate misinformation, or publishing another
person's private information without permission is not acceptable.

## Reporting

To report a conduct concern, contact the corresponding author listed in `CITATION.cff`. Reports
will be handled confidentially where possible and reviewed fairly. Project maintainers may remove
content, limit participation, or take other appropriate action in response to a violation.
