# Benchmark review checklist

When reviewing a new analysis or report, confirm the following before treating
it as part of the benchmark evidence:

- The protocol name and split provenance are stated explicitly.
- Any fitted transformation is trained only on the permitted training data.
- Representation, learner, and evaluation settings remain comparable across
  the models being discussed.
- Historical or external results are labelled separately from the primary
  clean-protocol evidence.
- Confidence intervals, correction procedures, and known provenance caveats
  are retained in the report.
